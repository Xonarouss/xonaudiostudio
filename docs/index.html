Comprehensive Suite

Engineered for Creative Sovereignty

Every tool you need to clone voices, engineer sound design, and manage large neural weights cleanly on Windows.

Voice Lab (F5-TTS)

Upload any 5–10 second voice sample (MP3, WAV, M4A) and clone timbre, tone, and pacing without any model fine-tuning. Non-autoregressive flow matching ensures smooth prosody and zero word repetition.

  • Speed scaling from 0.6x to 1.6x
  • Silence auto-trimming & normalization
  • SoundFile fallback (no FFmpeg DLL errors)

Sound Studio (Stable Audio)

Generate high-resolution stereo audio at full 44.1kHz. Perfect for film foley, gaming soundscapes, transitions, weapons, ambient weather, and musical motifs from 1 up to 47 continuous seconds.

  • 1 to 47 seconds variable duration slider
  • CFG Guidance scale & step tuning
  • Reproducible random & fixed seeds

Llama 3 AI Co-Pilot & Wand

Equipped with a built-in local Llama 3 engine. Click the Magic Wand (🪄) next to any text field to transform simple one-word ideas into rich, high-yield acoustic prompts. Plus, chat directly with an audio engineer bot.

  • 1-Click Magic Wand Prompt Refiner (🪄)
  • Model-check detection with friendly warnings
  • Floating chat co-pilot for studio advice

Real-Time Telemetry

Always know what your hardware is doing. Features an ultra-accurate floating hardware monitor displaying GPU utilization, temperature (°C), VRAM usage, total system RAM, CPU %, and engine footprint.

  • Native NVML NVIDIA driver queries
  • Polled every 2.5s with zero overhead
  • Unified matching width & collapsible

Storage Reclaim & Updates

AI weights can occupy massive SSD capacity. XonAudio Studio lets you inspect exact on-disk model sizes in gigabytes and wipe them in 1 click whenever you need to free up storage space.

  • 1-Click Clean Storage button
  • Auto-checks HF commit SHAs for updates
  • Auto-downloads all models on BAT launch

Generations Archive

Every audio file you produce is safely cataloged in your local library. Play clips directly from the archive, inspect prompts, download WAV files, or delete old recordings with full confidentiality.

  • Auto-indexed history with timestamps
  • Direct WAV export & batch cleaning
  • No cloud sync, completely private
